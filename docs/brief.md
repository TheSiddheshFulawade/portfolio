## Goal
Get interviews for engineering roles at global banks and their technology centres
(Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, remote), then make those interviews easier by giving the
interviewer concrete things to ask about.

## Audience and what each needs

| Hiring manager | 5 minutes | Settlement Lab write-up, decisions and trade-offs, experience |
| Interviewer | 30 minutes | Settlement Lab running live, public code, specs, ADRs, tests |

## Positioning
"I build payment systems that stay correct under load."
Employer work is shown as experience only. Settlement Lab, an original open-source
settlement engine, is the flagship project and the technical proof.

## Tone
Plain, specific, confident without hype. Numbers over adjectives. No "passionate",
no "rockstar", no skill-percentage bars. International number format (100,000 not 1,00,000).

## Look and feel
An observability console for a payment engine, applied to a career. Dark-first with a
light theme, one teal accent plus amber for warnings, monospace for numbers. Clean,
generous spacing, purposeful motion (message flow, live charts, view transitions).

## Must have
- Home, Experience, Projects, Settlement Lab (live), Notes, About, Contact, Engineering page
- Content editable without code (content/ files, /admin CMS, /add-content command)
- Resume PDF generated from the same content
- Visitor analytics (private) and a public visitor counter
- A+ security headers, Lighthouse ≥95 mobile, WCAG 2.2 AA
